## Grace M. Adjeli

**AI Governance & Security Researcher | PhD Candidate | CISSP**

I work on the gap between AI governance and the systems that have to enforce it.

Most AI governance lives in frameworks and policy documents. My work is about what happens at runtime, the moment a decision actually executes. I research and build the **Execution Governance Model**, a decision-centric framework for AI admissibility at runtime, and I practice **AIGRC Engineering**: turning governance, risk, and compliance from documented policy into deployable infrastructure.

Background in DevSecOps. Currently completing my doctorate at Capitol Technology University, with a thesis on ethical frameworks for human-AI collaboration in cybersecurity decision-making.
